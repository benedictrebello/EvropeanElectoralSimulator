Evropean Assembly

Seats go only to lists that clear the electoral threshold. A list that falls short has its votes split across every qualifying list, weighted by ideological closeness on the left-right spectrum — the nearer a list, the bigger its share. Seats are then allocated by the method chosen in Configuration. Swing is measured against each list's "Prev %" baseline — for an alliance, that's the combined baseline of its current member parties.
